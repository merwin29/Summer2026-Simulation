Important caveat used in slides
-------------------------------
Stage 12 is presented as a first physics/robot bridge. It is not presented as a final validated OEM-grade haul-truck dynamic model. The current full-scale side uses a fixed loaded mass and a stable loaded-outbound Simscape slice; active braking/retarding is wired but not yet active in the first stable implementation.
